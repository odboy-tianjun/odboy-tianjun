I'm an ordinary programmer, but a hardcore tinkerer: I have strong self-learning and problem-solving abilities, and a bit of a code cleanliness obsession. I care more about doing things right and doing them well than just getting them done.

- **Catchphrase**: Life is a continuous process of trial and error, and so are projects. Keep trying, keep improving. With a burning determination, one day it will appear before the world.

### My Programming Journey

I got into computers relatively late — I didn't own my first laptop until after the college entrance exam. My first programming language was C. Later, I used various languages and got into game cheats, reverse engineering, network protocols (especially cryptographic analysis), and automation scripts. I've modeled GTA5 car assets in 3DsMax9 and written plenty of small programs in BAT scripts.

Now, purely writing business logic has become somewhat tedious for me, and I want to recapture that original drive for tinkering. Maybe I'll switch tracks: AI large models? iOS development and reverse engineering? Rust? Hardware development? The point is to keep myself busy — only busyness makes each day fulfilling and meaningful.
